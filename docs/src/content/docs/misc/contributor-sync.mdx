Join the LiveStore community to discuss contributions, share progress, and work through open questions.
We meet every two weeks for one hour, normally on Thursday at 19:00 Europe/Berlin.
The time stays at 19:00 in Berlin when daylight saving time changes.

This page is the canonical meeting information source. Check it for the latest dates and any cancellation or rescheduling notes.
Schedule changes normally appear here within a few minutes.

## Keeping your calendar up to date

Subscribe to the LiveStore calendar to receive future meetings and schedule changes in your calendar app.
Subscriptions may refresh slowly, and calendar publishing can occasionally lag behind this page.

**Adding one meeting or downloading its calendar file creates a copy that does not update automatically.**
Check this page before joining, especially if you saved a meeting earlier.

You can use the same [Riverside meeting room](https://docs.livestore.dev/meet) for every contributor sync.

The [LiveStore contributor chat on Discord](https://discord.com/channels/1154415661842452532/1344991859805786142) is the canonical place for contributor discussion between meetings.

Find recordings of previous meetings on the [community page](/misc/community/#livestore-contributor-sync).
